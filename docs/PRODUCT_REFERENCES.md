## What the cutaways explain

The exterior models use the published photographs and envelope information. Internal layouts are functional teaching sections, not unpublished manufacturer CAD. No literal PCB layout, code-disk track count, spring geometry or number of SR085 circuits is asserted.

- **KIS50:** rotating disk and shaft, fixed scanning fork and electronics. The PCB and cover are sectioned to keep the disk visible. Displayed pulses still derive from the original 1024 PPR demonstration state.
- **RIM2000:** magnetic ring rotates with the internal motor shaft; the sensing head and cable stay fixed. Copper windings and a partial motor shell provide context. A programmable 1024 PPR example retains the existing demonstration's feedback relationship.
- **D125:** cable winding follows the existing payout calculation; the return spring and magnetic pickup explain the functional chain. Verified cable endpoints, reference payout and drum radius remain unchanged.
- **SR085:** the cutaway opens the viewing window while contacts remain closed. Exploding the contacts is a separate operation and disconnects the rotating loads.

## Installation checks

The wheel touches the belt at the declared 300 mm rolling circumference and clears the parcel lane. The pivot is attached to a frame bracket and the encoder flange is carried by the spring arm. Motor feedback fits within the motor shell instead of extending behind the fan cover.

The carousel uses an independent bearing and adapter to carry the platform. The fixed supply, stator torque restraint and rotating output occupy the drive pedestal. No fixed service mast crosses the moving horses. Pedestal and deck service cuts reveal the installation during inspection. Supply and output cable curves are checked for ground clearance.

Some legacy PDF links on the manufacturer's site redirected to its document finder during review. The app uses local modeled geometry and does not depend on those downloads at runtime. Product configuration and internal teaching details should be reviewed with Kübler before any claim of manufacturer-approved mounting or application certification.
